VeriLiv V2 — Quantitative Liver MRI · Disclaimer v1.0

01Purpose

This worksheet converts quantitative liver MRI measurements — PDFF, R2*/LIC, MRE stiffness, native T1 and cT1, ADC — into guideline-referenced stages, and shows the provenance of every cut-off it applies. Its aim is educational and organisational.

02Acquisition scope & what transfers

Thresholds are field-strength (1.5 T / 3.0 T), cohort (adult / paediatric) and measurement-technique dependent. On the GE clinical-product path the technique is determined by the products themselves (IDEAL-IQ, MR Touch, StarMap), so cut-off attribution is exact. On the other-or-unspecified path the cut-offs derive from guideline values and vendor-neutral literature, and may not transfer to a given scanner or sequence without local validation. Where no published cut-off exists for the technique you select, the report prints a described gap rather than a number. Values from non-standardised sequences — two-point Dixon fat fraction, a 40 Hz MRE driver, a different reconstruction — are not interchangeable with the confounder-corrected measurements these cut-offs were derived from.

04Intended user & clinical limits

For qualified healthcare professionals. It is an adjunct to — never a substitute for — interpretation by a radiologist. It performs no image analysis and applies no quality control to what you type: it cannot detect a failed acquisition, a mis-placed ROI, motion corruption, or an invalid confidence map. V2 is in development and must not be used to interpret patient data.

05Data protection & privacy

The report runs entirely in your browser. No measurement, identifier, or laboratory value is transmitted to any server, and nothing is written to durable storage — entered data lives only in this tab and is discarded when it closes. The sole exception is a non-personal record of this acknowledgement, held for the duration of the session. Web fonts may be requested from Google Fonts; block that if your environment requires it — the report falls back to system fonts and prints identically.

07Trial feedback

If you were given an invitation link, this page can report a problem to the trial's feedback form, which is hosted by Tally. What it sends: your invite code, the acquisition you selected, the band your reading fell in, and what you write. It never sends the measurements you typed, the accession number, the study date or the age. Without an invitation link nothing is sent and nothing is loaded.
