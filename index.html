How it's built

One report. However each number was measured.

A liver-MRI workup arrives in pieces — fat and iron from the scanner's own module, stiffness from a separate elastography package, the lab indices from the laboratory system. veri.Liver is the single page that takes all of them, staging each against the guidance that applies to how it was acquired and recording which device or method produced it. It is tied to none of the systems that produced the data.

You enter the numbers — there is no automated import. What the report adds is the staging, the provenance, and one printed page.

No proprietary score.

veri.Liver introduces no biomarker of its own. Every boundary a patient is staged against is a published, peer-reviewed cut-off or a consensus guideline, cited on the report — open the source and check the number against it. Nothing in the report depends on a value that exists only inside this tool: no index to license, no black box to take on trust.
