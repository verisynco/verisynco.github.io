03 How it's built

The reading, done once.

The liver-MRI literature is large, scattered, and shifting. Weighing all of it to a defensible stage, case by case, is not realistic for a non-subspecialist. veri.Liver is that synthesis — assembled with AI into one logical framework: the subspecialist reading, in one place.

Built with AI. Runs with none.

No model in the loop when a report is made. Every boundary resolves to a published cut-off you can open and check.
